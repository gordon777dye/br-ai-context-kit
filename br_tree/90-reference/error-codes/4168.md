**Error:** 4168

**Description:** The session reached for the operating system while an assistant was attached

**Details:** `SYSTEM` with a command, a spool command, a gated `CONFIG` operation, or `Ctrl-]` is refused while an assistant is attached. It is refused to the person at the session as well as to the assistant. The powerful `CONFIG` operations are refused outright with this error: they change what the session is, and there is no program to name in a question about them.

**Name:** `BREAIOPERATIONREFUSED`

**Source:** BR 5.0 `wberrdefs.h`, `BREAIOPERATIONREFUSED`: "the assistant may not reach the operating system: a grant lends the keyboard, not the shell".
