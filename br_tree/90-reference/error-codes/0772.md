**Error:** 0772

**Description:** HTTP port range must run low to high, at most 100 ports

**Name:** `BREHTTPCFGRANGE`

**Details:** A `<port>-<last>` range is backwards, too long, or runs past 65535. Raised as for 0770.

**SYSERR$:** The range.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §1 Configuration directives](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#setup)

> A range of up to 100 ports, lowest first:

**Fix:** Correct the range, such as `8081-8084`.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
