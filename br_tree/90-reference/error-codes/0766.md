**Error:** 0766

**Description:** CHUNK= must be 256 to 16777216

**Name:** `BREHTTPCHUNKRANGE`

**Details:** `CHUNK=` on an `HTTP=SERVER` OPEN is outside its range.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §2.1 Statements](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#interface)

> **Streams large responses in pieces of this many bytes** (256 to 16,777,216).

**Fix:** Give a size in that range. 32768 is a good default.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
