**Error:** 0795

**Description:** TLS refused the certificate or key - see SYSERR$

**Name:** `BREHTTPSREFUSED`

**Details:** Both files load, and OpenSSL refuses them, such as a key that is too small.

**SYSERR$:** OpenSSL's reason.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §3.1 Prerequisites](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#tls)

> RSA or EC (ECDSA), encrypted or not, and strong enough for OpenSSL's default security level.

**Fix:** Get a certificate and key that meet the requirement.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
