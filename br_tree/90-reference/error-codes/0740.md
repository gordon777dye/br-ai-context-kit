**Error:** 0740

**Description:** This BR already has an `HTTP=SERVER` file open
Available as of BR! `4.30`.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §2.1 Statements](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#interface)

> Only **one** channel may hold `HTTP=SERVER` at a time in one BR: a second OPEN gives error **740**.

**Fix:** Use the channel already open, or `CLOSE` it first.
