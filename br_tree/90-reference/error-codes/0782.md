**Error:** 0782

**Description:** HTTPS needs CERT= naming `<base>` for `<base>.crt` and `<base>.key`

**Name:** `BREHTTPSNOCERT`

**Details:** `CONFIG HTTPS` turned HTTPS on, and no `CERT=` was given on either line.

**SYSERR$:** The HTTPS port.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §1 Configuration directives](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#setup)

> Required with `HTTPS`.

**Fix:** Add `CERT=<basename>`. `web_server.br` takes it from `CERT=` in `Web_Server.INI`.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
