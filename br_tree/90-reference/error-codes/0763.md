**Error:** 0763

**Description:** HTTP= must be SERVER or CLIENT

**Name:** `BREHTTPTYPE`

**Details:** The OPEN string has `HTTP=` with another value.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §2.1 Statements](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#interface)

> `HTTP=` is `SERVER` or `CLIENT`.

**Fix:** Write `HTTP=SERVER` to serve requests, or `HTTP=CLIENT` to make them.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
