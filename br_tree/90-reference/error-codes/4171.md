**Error:** 4171

**Description:** A line the assistant sent used the `AI` command

**Details:** Two causes. A turn is the person's to start, and a reply is how one ends. A line the assistant sent that runs `AI`, including `AI NEW`, is refused with this error. Also raised when a turn is already running and two requests are already waiting behind it: a request made during a turn waits (up to two), and the fourth is refused. See [4169](4169.md).

**Name:** `BREAITURNNOTYOURS`

**Source:** BR 5.0 `wberrdefs.h`, `BREAITURNNOTYOURS`: "this AI request was not started: a turn is the person's to start, and only two may wait behind the one running".
