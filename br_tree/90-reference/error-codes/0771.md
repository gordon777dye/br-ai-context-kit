**Error:** 0771

**Description:** CONFIG HTTP or HTTPS needs a port from 1 to 65535

**Name:** `BREHTTPCFGPORT`

**Details:** The port is missing, is not a number, or is out of range. The port comes first on the line. Raised as for 0770.

**SYSERR$:** The word found instead.

**Setup documentation:** [HTTP_SERVER.md §1 Configuration directives](../../60-integration/web/HTTP_SERVER.md#setup)

> A port is 1 to 65535.

**Fix:** Put a valid port straight after `HTTP` or `HTTPS`.

Available as of BR! `5.02`.
